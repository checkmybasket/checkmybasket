# Five guides for morning review

Status: Owner approved publication on 24 October 2026. These Markdown files retain the original draft/review record. The five articles are now implemented under their proposed app routes, with metadata from lib/editorial-guides.ts and discovery through the guide index/sitemap. Draft status lines and editorial review notes are not rendered publicly. Deployment verification follows the publication commit.

The topics target distinct reader problems rather than duplicate existing budget, office, remote-team or wishlist guides. Search demand and competitiveness have not been measured; these are editorial hypotheses, not keyword-volume claims. No invented tests, personal experience, prices, stock, testimonials or app features are used.

Suggested review order:

1. [Secret Santa tomorrow](01-secret-santa-tomorrow.md): urgent planning intent, distinct from a generic product list.
2. [Copyable gift questionnaire](02-secret-santa-questionnaire.md): practical resource with a clear path into wishlists.
3. [No-clutter gifts](03-no-clutter-secret-santa.md): recipient preference rather than another demographic gift list.
4. [Secondhand Secret Santa](04-secondhand-secret-santa.md): group rules, condition checks and a specific exchange theme.
5. [Anonymous wrapping](05-anonymous-secret-santa-wrapping.md): creative finishing guidance and clue/privacy pitfalls.

Each draft includes proposed title/description/slug, intent, a complete article and contextual links to existing pages. Publication preparation should validate copy and any factual updates, implement normal metadata, confirm the live UI matches product claims and check mobile layout. Add genuine owner attribution and original photos if available; do not invent them. Reassess overlap using Search Console after publication, rather than assuming five new pages will improve rankings.

Research context: Google's people-first content guidance favours useful, original value over volume. WWF suggests secondhand, homemade and experience gifts; these drafts do not attach numerical environmental savings to those choices. Sources: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content ; https://www.wwf.org.uk/challenges/gifts-dont-cost-world . Source-inspired material is paraphrased briefly; the practical scenarios and templates are original editorial drafts.
